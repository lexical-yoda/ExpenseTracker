A read-only progress view — no auto-debit, no penalties for skipped months.
How this works
  1. Set a monthly bucket allocation in Settings → Investment Plan — e.g. "₹60k to equity, ₹30k to a fun fund"
  2. Tag Income or Transfer transactions on Manage to a bucket — it auto-selects when the account matches
  3. This Month below shows live progress; Since Plan Start only counts a month once it's actually over — a brand-new plan starts at zero, not a phantom month's worth of "already due"
  4. No auto-debit, no reminders, no penalty for a skipped month — this is a mirror, not a budgeting cop
